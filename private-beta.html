Private beta FAQ

The important boundaries, in plain language.

Does applying give me dashboard access?

No. Applying starts a qualification conversation with Blake. Selected testers receive a separate invitation only after the connected data path, account controls, and required release gates pass.

Will the v0.6 beta stop or approve agent actions?

No. Authority is NONE and enforcement is OFF. The beta observes supported activity, creates non-authoritative proposals, diagnoses governability, and prepares evidence for human review.

Is there a public signup or paid plan?

No. There is no public self-service account flow, billing, payment processing, or enforcement credit in this beta. Access is invite-only and manually approved.

Do I need the full 24-hour canary?

Not by default. Selected testers complete a bounded preflight and short observation proof. A longer validation window may be requested for the first design partners, elevated environments, or material upgrades.

What can I use without an account?

The current public OpenClaw plugin supports local shadow use without a connected dashboard account. Connected access is a separate, manually approved beta path.

Does the beta certify compliance or prove every outcome?

No. Coverage depends on supported hooks, mappings, evidence, and environment context. Unknown, incomplete, conflicting, or stale evidence remains unknown and must not be presented as proof.
