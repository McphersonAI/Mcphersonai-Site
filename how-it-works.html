How It Works

Your workflow.
A boundary you can inspect.

McPherson AI provides software and scoped qualification around the implementation you already operate. Your team keeps the workflow and the client relationship.

Who owns what.

You own the implementation

Your workflow, runtime and automation, client relationships, customer-specific mapping and configuration, credentials, and customer authorization stay with you.

We provide the governance layer

We define the workflow boundary with you. Observa records runtime evidence and evaluates it in SHADOW. Outcome checks depend on what the system of record exposes.

A written scope, with limits

Qualification records the workflow version, environment, period, success criteria and findings. You receive a report of what ran, what governance would have decided, and which outcomes could be checked.

We do not build or rewrite your workflow, take control of your agent, or take custody of your system credentials. Observa installation credentials are separate from the credentials your workflow uses.

Qualification starts with written criteria.

1. Connect your implementation

Use the supported OpenClaw or Local Node/n8n path. Keep ownership of your workflow and the systems it touches.

2. Run in SHADOW

Agree on written success criteria and use a synthetic or authorized staging environment. Review actions, counterfactual decisions and evidence gaps.

3. Choose commercial deployment

When the agreed criteria are met, you choose whether to continue. A paid subscription begins for each live client deployment.

Qualification means your implementation ran in SHADOW and met success criteria agreed in writing for a stated workflow version, environment and period. It is not a certification, guarantee or compliance determination.

Make the boundary concrete.

  • Which actions and systems belong to this workflow?
  • Which actions are out of scope or require authorization in your existing systems?
  • What runtime evidence is available, and what is missing?
  • Which outcome can be checked against the system of record?
  • Which workflow version, runtime configuration, environment and evaluation period are covered?

What continues in a live deployment.

The subscription covers ongoing evidence, outcome checks where available, version tracking and continued governance coverage within the agreed deployment scope. Each live client deployment has its own subscription.

Reusing a workflow boundary does not automatically qualify every change or customer configuration. The report states what was evaluated and what remains outside scope.
