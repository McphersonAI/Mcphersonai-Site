Three truths. Keep them separate.

AUTHORIZED != COMPLETED != VERIFIED

AUTHORIZED

Did the action have the required authorization? An observed action or successful outcome cannot establish that on its own.

COMPLETED

The runtime reports that the action completed. That does not show that the intended business outcome happened.

VERIFIED

A specific outcome check found supporting system-of-record evidence within its scope. It does not prove authorization or the correctness of the whole workflow.

A governance decision is not a runtime intervention.

For mapped actions with sufficient context, SHADOW decisions describe what policy would decide: WOULD_ALLOW, WOULD_DENY or WOULD_REQUIRE_APPROVAL. Other states preserve missing context or inability to decide.

WOULD_DENY does not mean anything was blocked. Unmapped activity does not automatically receive a governance decision. A completion record is runtime evidence, not an authorization decision.
