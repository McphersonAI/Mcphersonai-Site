Reporting boundary

The report should never outrun the evidence.

Observa can make a case easier to review, but presentation quality does not expand observation coverage or prove an unobserved outcome.

What a report can do

  • Organize attempts, completions, policy results, and source evidence
  • Surface contradictions, weak evidence, and unresolved review items
  • Distinguish directly measured findings from guidance

What a report cannot assume

  • That missing evidence means no action occurred
  • That every tool or connected application was observed
  • That a policy result controlled execution
  • That a readable report is a compliance or safety certification
