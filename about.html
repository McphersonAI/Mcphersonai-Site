About McPherson AI

Start with the work.
Then define the boundary.

McPherson AI is a founder-led company building governance and evidence around repeatable AI workflows.

Blake McPherson, founder.

McPherson AI began by turning sixteen years of frontline restaurant operations into eight repeatable AI-supported workflow products. The work ranged from daily operating review to labor, food cost, shift handoffs and weekly business reporting.

That experience shaped a practical product instinct: AI becomes useful when it is attached to real, repeated business work. As the workflows became more capable, the harder question was how to define what they should be allowed to do and how an operator could know what happened.

The workflow is the smallest useful governable business surface.

An agent can use many tools. A workflow sold to a client has a particular job, specific systems and an accountable owner. That is a concrete place to define a boundary, collect evidence and check a result.

That question led to Observa. Today, McPherson AI works with builders and agencies that already own the implementation and client relationship. Our role is the governance, evidence and scoped outcome-checking layer around consequential actions.

Domain experience is the origin, not a product claim.

The QSR workflows were not governed by Observa. Their distribution does not establish customer usage, business value or revenue. The story matters because it explains why we start with the workflow and its owner.
