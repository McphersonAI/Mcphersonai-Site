Founding Builder Program

You keep your implementation.
We qualify the governance layer around it.

For builders and agencies already deploying one AI workflow to multiple paying customers. Bring a repeated business action and the system of record that can tell you whether it happened.

Apply as a Founding Builder

A defined evaluation. A clear decision.

Connect through a supported interface and run in SHADOW against synthetic data or a properly authorized test or staging environment. Together, we define the workflow boundary and agree success criteria in writing.

The qualification report records what ran, what governance would have decided and, where your system of record allows, whether outcomes matched what the runtime reported.

Qualification means your implementation ran in SHADOW and met success criteria agreed in writing for a stated workflow version, environment and period. It is not a certification, guarantee or compliance determination.

SHADOW ONLY · AUTHORITY NONE · ENFORCEMENT OFF

Qualification has no fee.

During qualification

Founding qualification is free, with no separate activation charge. An application does not create a subscription or authorize testing.

After qualification

Payment begins only if the agreed criteria are met and you choose commercial deployment. A paid subscription applies per live client deployment approved under separately agreed written terms before deployment.

If the workflow does not qualify, or you choose not to deploy, the evaluation simply ends. Subscription pricing is agreed during scoping; no payment is collected on this website.

A good fit starts with real repeated work.

  • A repeatable workflow, preferably already deployed to multiple paying clients.
  • A consequential action and an identifiable system of record.
  • A common workflow shape across customers, with the differences understood.
  • A technical owner and a business/workflow owner.
  • Synthetic or staging access, or explicit authorization for the proposed test scope.

Tell us about one workflow.

We are accepting a small number of founding builders. Give enough detail to assess fit without identifying your clients. Client names and confidential evidence are never required.

Do not submit secrets, credentials, tokens, PHI, payment data, customer records, message bodies or confidential client artifacts. Read the privacy notice.

What happens after you apply.

  • A human reviews the application for scope and fit. There is no guaranteed response time.
  • If there is a fit, a scoping conversation identifies the workflow boundary, owners and test environment.
  • Success criteria are agreed in writing before SHADOW evaluation.
  • You review the findings and decide whether to continue into commercial deployment.
