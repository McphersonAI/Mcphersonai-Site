Governance Analysis

What policy would have decided, while staying in SHADOW.

Available now in Hosted Observa for evaluated activity. A dedicated walkthrough of these states is in production; until then, here is exactly what the words mean.

  • WOULD_ALLOWAn enforcing policy would have permitted this action.
  • WOULD_DENYIt would have refused — and the action still ran, because nothing is enforced.
  • WOULD_REQUIRE_APPROVALIt would have held this for a human. Nothing was held.

Counterfactual by construction. Not every runtime action receives a decision; activity never becomes a decision on its own, and unevaluated activity stays visible as UNEVALUATED.

The accountability problem

A successful runtime response is not proof the work happened.

An agent can report success while the downstream system tells a different story. A log entry can be useful without answering the questions an operator, reviewer, or incident owner actually needs resolved.

  • A completion may not proveThe correct action occurred.
  • A completion may not provePolicy was followed.
  • A completion may not proveApproval was required or obtained.
  • A completion may not proveThe intended downstream state exists.
  • A completion may not proveThe evidence is sufficient.
  • A completion may not proveThe issue is resolved.

How the governed path works

A readable path from action to verified reality.

Each stage has a distinct job. The current beta observes, records, and verifies; it does not give any policy decision authority over execution.

  1. Agent actsSupported activity enters the observed path.
  2. Observa observesAttempts and completions are recorded with minimized metadata.
  3. Policy evaluatesMapped activity is checked against explicit rules in shadow mode.
  4. Evidence is boundAttempt, decision, and observed completion are correlated.
  5. Reality is checkedSupported outcomes are compared with authoritative system state.
  6. Operator reviewsVerified results, mismatches, and unknowns stay distinct.
Current beta boundary

What this beta does, and what it does not

What it does

  • Discovers agents, tools, and capabilities on supported n8n and OpenClaw paths
  • Generates mappings for human review and classification
  • Captures structured, reviewable governance evidence
  • Evaluates policy in shadow mode
  • Independently verifies supported workflow outcomes against authoritative system state
  • Keeps mismatches and uncertainty visible instead of assuming success

What it does not do

  • Does not block, approve, deny, or rewrite agent actions
  • Does not take control of production systems
  • Does not treat a runtime completion as proof of the downstream state
  • Does not claim every workflow can be automatically verified
  • Does not certify compliance, completeness, or safety
