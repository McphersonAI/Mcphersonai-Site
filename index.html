The run finished. Did the work happen?

Imagine a workflow that updates a client’s CRM. The runtime reports success, but the intended field in the CRM has not changed. The run completed; the business outcome is still unconfirmed. This is an illustrative failure, not a customer case study.

AUTHORIZED != COMPLETED != VERIFIED

Permission to act, a completed tool call and a confirmed business outcome answer different questions.

A broad agent needs a specific workflow boundary.

Define the work

Name the business purpose, allowed actions, systems in scope and actions outside the boundary.

Define the evidence

Specify the records needed to understand what ran and which outcome the system of record can confirm.

Define the scope

State the workflow version and environment. A finding applies to that scope, not every customer configuration.

What Observa shows you today.

The public OpenClaw CLI separates completed tool activity from SHADOW decisions. Its local views expose evidence warnings and bounded history; they do not turn a completed tool call into a verified business outcome.

Qualify the pattern you deploy repeatedly.

You may sell the same business workflow to several clients. We work with you to qualify the governance boundary around that repeated workflow type, so the structure can be reused within its stated version and environment limits.

You retain the implementation and customer-specific configuration. A changed runtime, mapping or workflow can change what the qualification covers.

Qualification means your implementation ran in SHADOW and met success criteria agreed in writing for a stated workflow version, environment and period. It is not a certification, guarantee or compliance determination.

How it works

From your implementation to a scoped deployment.

1. Connect your implementation

Use the supported OpenClaw or Local Node/n8n path. Keep ownership of your workflow and the systems it touches.

2. Run in SHADOW

Agree on written success criteria and use a synthetic or authorized staging environment. Review actions, counterfactual decisions and evidence gaps.

3. Choose commercial deployment

When the agreed criteria are met, you choose whether to continue. A paid subscription begins for each live client deployment.

Observe first. Keep authority with your systems.

SHADOW ONLY · AUTHORITY NONE · ENFORCEMENT OFF

Observa records and evaluates. It has no authority to block, approve or rewrite your workflow. Your existing authorization and approval controls remain your responsibility.

SHADOW describes authority. It does not mean zero latency or zero technical impact. Evaluate the integration in staging.
