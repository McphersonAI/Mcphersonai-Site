Privacy

Website privacy notice.

This notice describes the behavior of this static website and its email-based application path. Hosted Observa has a separate onboarding agreement.

What this website does.

The website serves static pages and assets. Its site code does not set analytics cookies, use browser storage, or load third-party analytics. Hosting and network providers receive the connection information needed to serve a request; this site does not state a verified hosting-log retention period.

External package, source and registry links take you to services with their own policies. The Founding application is prepared in your browser. The site code sends no application request to a server. If you choose email, your email provider handles the message you send.

What you choose to send.

The Founding application asks for contact details, your role and non-sensitive information about the workflow and its deployment. Review the prepared message before sending. McPherson AI receives the information by email to assess the inquiry and respond.

Do not send secrets, credentials, tokens, PHI, payment data, customer records, message bodies or confidential client artifacts. Client names are not required. You may instead send a short, non-sensitive inquiry directly to admin@mcphersonai.com.

Questions about information you have shared.

Contact admin@mcphersonai.com to discuss access, correction or deletion of information sent to McPherson AI. This notice does not establish a retention period, deletion deadline or backup-erasure commitment.

Before sending data to Hosted Observa, review the actual evaluation agreement and confirm its data, retention and deletion terms. The Trust page identifies facts not established by current public package artifacts.
