Founder background

The QSR origin.

Sixteen years of frontline restaurant operations became eight repeatable AI-supported workflow products. This is founder-domain experience, not an Observa deployment claim.

Repeated operating problems shaped the method.

  • Daily operations review and pre-rush planning.
  • Labor leak auditing, food cost diagnostics and ghost inventory.
  • Shift reflection, handoffs and audit-readiness routines.
  • Weekly profit-and-loss storytelling.

The work began with real operating questions, a source of truth and an accountable owner. That workflow-first discipline now informs how McPherson AI defines governance boundaries.

What this history establishes.

The QSR workflows were not governed by Observa. No download count is presented here as customers, adoption, revenue or business value. The current commercial offer is the Founding Builder Program.
