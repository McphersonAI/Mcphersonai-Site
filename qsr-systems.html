Built from the floor

Operating pain translated into practical review systems.

Food cost and waste

Ordering, portioning, receiving, waste, ghost inventory, and weekly variance patterns that usually show up after the week is already spent.

Labor leaks

Mid-week labor drift, scheduling assumptions, rush coverage, clock behavior, and the gap between planned hours and actual volume.

Shift execution

Opening, pre-rush positioning, mid-shift checks, close, unresolved issues, and handoffs that stop dying between shifts.
