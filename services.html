The process

A disciplined Workflow Map comes before a fixed build promise.

01

Consult. Start with one real workflow that is taking too much time, getting missed, or depending on memory.

02

Map. Document current steps, people, source of truth, failure points, approvals, sensitive-data boundaries, and what should not be automated.

03

Check access. Confirm vendor, API, export, and integration realities before making a delivery commitment.

04

Define the accountability scope. State what the system may do, what requires approval, what is logged, and what remains outside scope.

05

Build and prove. Start narrowly, test safely, review evidence, and expand only after the first workflow works as intended.
