Historical schema · June 2026

Audit Mode Schema

The original v0.1 accountability-case draft is retained for reference. It is not the current runtime evidence API, a benchmark or an external audit.

Known facts and unknowns remain separate.

The draft grouped available records into cases with known facts, unknowns, source references and follow-up items. It separated measured records from guidance. The historical blocked_action label describes source records; it does not mean Observa blocked an action. Current product behavior is described in Observa and canonical limitations.

Section A / Section B rule

Measured evidence and general guidance never mix.

Section A — Measured

Reconstructed from records. If a claim is measured from evidence, it belongs here.

Section B — Guidance

Advice, interpretation, or general best practice. Not measured from records.

This separation keeps evidence-backed reconstruction distinct from general recommendations. Audit Mode must not blend the two.

Design principle

Every case states six things.

  • What happened.
  • What is known.
  • What is unknown.
  • What evidence supports the case.
  • What needs owner review.
  • What should not be overclaimed.
